# Detailed Planner Run: Vegetarian Spring Week

Week: Tuesday, May 5 through Monday, May 11, 2026.

Household: 2 people.

Style: vegetarian at home, eggs and dairy assumed okay, rare optional pepperoni flex only on pizza.

Operating goal: one afternoon/evening cooking session per day. Breakfasts and lunches should be assembled from prior work.

## System View

This is not seven unrelated dinners. It is a week built from five shared systems:

1. Master dough.
2. Herb handling.
3. Crunchy vegetable handling.
4. Legume handling.
5. Sauce handling.

The planner should decide these first, then fill dinners, lunches, snack boxes, and breakfasts around them.

## Yield Targets

- 7 dinners for 2.
- 4 to 5 assembled lunches.
- 4 to 5 snack boxes.
- 6 to 8 breakfast jars/bowls.
- Minimal morning cooking.
- No more than one major appliance/station focus per day.

## Core Component Batches

### Batch 1: Master Lean Dough

Use for flatbreads, pita-style dippers, Ooni pizzas, and pita chips.

This is a practical hybrid dough. It is not a perfect Neapolitan pizza dough and not a perfect pita dough, but it is good enough to function as the week carrier.

Ingredients:

- 750 g bread flour or all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting.
- 500 g cool water.
- 15 g fine salt.
- 15 g olive oil.
- 2 g instant yeast.

Tuesday method:

1. Mix flour, water, and yeast until no dry flour remains.
2. Rest 20 minutes.
3. Add salt and olive oil. Knead or fold until incorporated.
4. Rest 30 minutes, then do one stretch-and-fold.
5. Rest 30 minutes, then do one more stretch-and-fold.
6. Refrigerate overnight in a lightly oiled covered container.

Wednesday handling:

1. Divide into:
   - 6 small balls, 90 g each, for flatbreads/dippers.
   - 3 larger balls, 220 g each, for Ooni pizzas/flatbreads.
   - Any remainder becomes pita chips or a test flatbread.
2. Store each ball in a lightly oiled deli container or covered tray.
3. Use small balls Wednesday through Friday.
4. Use large balls Saturday.

Quality rule:

- Dough is best days 1 to 4 after mixing.
- If it over-ferments by Saturday, use it for rustic flatbreads instead of delicate pizza.
- Pull small flatbread balls 45 to 60 minutes before cooking.
- Pull Ooni balls 2 hours before firing.

### Batch 2: Herb System

Buy:

- 1 large bunch parsley.
- 1 bunch mint.
- 1 bunch dill.
- 1 bunch cilantro, or a second bunch parsley if cilantro is not wanted.
- 2 bunches scallions or spring onions.

Tuesday handling:

1. Wash all herbs.
2. Spin or towel-dry very thoroughly. Wet herbs rot quickly.
3. Pick tender leaves from tough stems.
4. Save tender stems separately.

Make three stored states:

#### H1: Chopped Mediterranean Herb Mix

Yield: about 2 cups.

Mix:

- 1 cup chopped parsley.
- 1/3 cup chopped mint.
- 1/3 cup chopped dill.
- 1/3 cup chopped cilantro or extra parsley.

Storage:

- Store in a deli quart lined with dry paper towel.
- Add a second dry paper towel on top.
- Label: `H1 chopped herb mix, use Tue-Fri`.

Uses:

- Tuesday: 3 tbsp over cabbage/asparagus.
- Wednesday: 1/4 cup in frittata and salad.
- Thursday: 1/2 cup in herb yogurt.
- Friday: 2 tbsp over noodles.
- Remaining: snack boxes or sauce.

#### H2: Whole Leaf Herb Reserve

Yield: 1 to 2 loose cups.

Storage:

- Leave whole.
- Wrap in dry paper towel.
- Store in a deli quart or zip bag.
- Label: `H2 whole herb leaves, use Sat-Mon`.

Uses:

- Saturday pizza finish.
- Sunday lentil salad.
- Monday rescue bowls.

Rule:

- Do not chop these on Tuesday. Whole leaves hold better. On later days, tear by hand or cut with scissors directly over food. This avoids another board session while preserving quality.

#### H3: Herb Stems And Scallion Trim

Yield: 1 loose cup.

Storage:

- Store chopped or rough-cut stems and scallion greens in a deli cup.
- Label: `H3 stems for sauce, use Thu`.

Uses:

- Thursday herb yogurt.
- Optional green sauce.

### Batch 3: Crunchy Vegetable System

Buy:

- 2 English cucumbers or 5 to 6 Persian cucumbers.
- 2 bunches radishes.
- 1 small red onion, optional.

Tuesday handling:

1. Wash cucumbers and radishes.
2. Trim radishes.
3. Keep half the cucumbers whole for Saturday onward if using English cucumbers. Fully sliced cucumber gets watery after 3 days.

Make three states:

#### CV1: Quick Pickles

Yield: about 3 cups.

Ingredients:

- 1 English cucumber, sliced, or 3 Persian cucumbers.
- 1 bunch radishes, sliced.
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced, optional.
- 3/4 cup vinegar.
- 3/4 cup water.
- 1 tbsp sugar or honey.
- 2 tsp kosher salt.
- 1 smashed garlic clove.
- Dill stems or 2 tbsp chopped dill.

Method:

1. Warm vinegar, water, sugar, and salt just enough to dissolve.
2. Pour over vegetables.
3. Cool, cover, and refrigerate.

Uses:

- Tuesday dinner.
- Wednesday lunch.
- Thursday mezze.
- Saturday pizza/snack boxes.
- Sunday lentil salad.

#### CV2: Raw Snack Sticks

Yield: 2 to 3 cups.

Ingredients:

- Remaining half cucumber, cut into sticks.
- A handful of radishes, halved or sliced.
- Snap peas if bought.

Storage:

- Deli quart with a damp paper towel on the bottom and dry paper towel on top.
- Use by Thursday.

#### CV3: Salted Salad Crunch

Yield: about 2 cups.

Ingredients:

- 1 cup sliced cucumber.
- 1 cup sliced radish.
- 1 sliced scallion.
- 1 tbsp lemon juice.
- 1 tbsp olive oil.
- Pinch salt.
- 2 tbsp H1 herb mix.

Use:

- Tuesday or Wednesday dinner.
- Do not hold more than 24 hours once salted.

### Batch 4: Lemon-Tahini Base

Yield: about 2 1/4 cups.

Ingredients:

- 1 cup tahini.
- 1/2 cup lemon juice.
- 2 garlic cloves, grated or pasted.
- 1 tsp kosher salt.
- 1/2 tsp cumin.
- 3/4 to 1 cup ice water.

Method:

1. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic, salt, and cumin.
2. It will seize. Slowly whisk in ice water until glossy and spoonable.
3. Store covered.

Storage:

- Label: `T1 lemon tahini base`.
- Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Uses:

- Tuesday dinner: 3/4 cup.
- Thursday hummus support: 1/4 cup if needed.
- Friday miso-tahini sauce: 1/2 cup.
- Sunday yogurt-tahini dressing: 1/2 cup.
- Monday rescue dressing: remainder.

### Batch 5: Chickpeas From Dry

Cook Wednesday.

Ingredients:

- 1 lb dry chickpeas.
- 7 cups water.
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
- 1 bay leaf, optional.

Instant Pot method:

1. Rinse chickpeas.
2. Add chickpeas, water, salt, and bay leaf to Instant Pot.
3. Cook high pressure 38 minutes.
4. Natural release 20 minutes, then vent.
5. Drain, reserving cooking liquid.

Expected yield:

- 6 to 7 cups cooked chickpeas.
- 2 to 3 cups aquafaba/cooking liquid.

Split:

- `CP1 hummus`: 3 cups chickpeas.
- `CP2 crispy`: 1 1/2 cups chickpeas, dried on a towel.
- `CP3 whole`: 1 1/2 cups chickpeas for salads/snack boxes.
- `CP4 buffer`: remaining chickpeas for Monday or freezer.
- `AQ1 aquafaba`: 1 to 2 cups cooking liquid.

Rule:

- Do not season the whole batch beyond light salt. The value is in keeping several neutral states.

### Batch 6: Hummus

Make Thursday.

Yield: about 4 cups.

Ingredients:

- 3 cups cooked chickpeas.
- 1/2 cup tahini.
- 1/3 cup lemon juice.
- 1 garlic clove.
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste.
- 1/2 tsp cumin.
- 1/2 cup aquafaba or ice water, plus more as needed.
- 2 tbsp olive oil.

Method:

1. In food processor, blend garlic, lemon, and salt for 30 seconds.
2. Add tahini and blend until thick.
3. Add chickpeas and cumin.
4. Stream in aquafaba or ice water until very smooth.
5. Finish with olive oil.

Split:

- Thursday dinner: 1 1/2 cups.
- Friday snack boxes: 1 cup.
- Saturday/Sunday snack boxes: 1 cup.
- Monday rescue: remainder.

### Batch 7: Herb Yogurt

Make Thursday after hummus, while the processor is already dirty.

Yield: about 2 1/2 cups.

Ingredients:

- 2 cups plain Greek or whole-milk yogurt.
- 1/2 cup H1 chopped herb mix.
- 2 tbsp H3 herb stems/scallion trim, optional.
- 1 tbsp lemon juice.
- 1 tbsp olive oil.
- 1 small garlic clove.
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt.
- Black pepper.

Method:

1. Pulse garlic and herb stems in the food processor.
2. Add yogurt, chopped herbs, lemon, oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Pulse just until green and combined.

Uses:

- Thursday dinner dip.
- Friday snack boxes.
- Sunday dressing base.
- Monday rescue.

### Batch 8: Crispy Chickpeas

Best made Friday or Sunday. If made Friday, refresh Sunday in a hot oven.

Yield: 1 1/2 cups.

Ingredients:

- 1 1/2 cups cooked chickpeas, very dry.
- 2 tbsp olive oil.
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika.
- 1/2 tsp cumin.
- Pinch chili flake.

Method:

1. Heat oven to 425 F.
2. Dry chickpeas aggressively with towels.
3. Toss with oil, salt, and spices.
4. Roast 25 to 35 minutes, shaking once or twice.
5. Cool uncovered.

Uses:

- Friday/Saturday crunch.
- Sunday lentil salad.
- Monday bowls.

### Batch 9: Lentils

Cook Sunday.

Ingredients:

- 1 1/4 cups dry French green, black, or brown lentils.
- 3 cups water.
- 1 bay leaf, optional.
- 1 tsp kosher salt.

Instant Pot method:

1. Rinse lentils.
2. Cook high pressure 8 minutes.
3. Quick release.
4. Drain if needed.
5. Dress while warm with 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, and a pinch of salt.

Expected yield:

- 3 to 3 1/2 cups cooked lentils.

Split:

- Sunday dinner: 2 cups.
- Monday lunch/dinner: 1 to 1 1/2 cups.

### Batch 10: Breakfast Jars

#### Strawberry Chia Jars

Make Tuesday.

Yield: 4 servings.

Ingredients:

- 1/2 cup chia seeds.
- 2 cups milk or alt milk.
- 1 cup yogurt.
- 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey.
- 1 tsp vanilla, optional.
- Pinch salt.
- 2 cups chopped strawberries.

Method:

1. Whisk chia, milk, yogurt, sweetener, vanilla, and salt.
2. Wait 10 minutes, whisk again.
3. Portion into 4 jars.
4. Top with strawberries.

Use:

- Wednesday and Thursday breakfasts.

#### Overnight Oats

Make Sunday.

Yield: 4 servings.

Ingredients:

- 2 cups rolled oats.
- 2 cups milk or alt milk.
- 1 cup yogurt.
- 2 tbsp chia seeds.
- 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey.
- Pinch salt.
- 2 cups strawberries, cherries, or apricots.
- Nuts or seeds for topping.

Use:

- Monday and Tuesday breakfasts.

## Daily Recipes And Execution

## Tuesday: Charred Cabbage And Asparagus With Lemon Tahini

Dinner output:

- Dinner for 2.
- 1 lunch portion.
- Chia jars for 2 days.
- Herb system started.
- Pickle system started.
- Master dough started.

Dinner ingredients:

- 1 small cabbage, cut into 6 wedges.
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed.
- 3 tbsp olive oil.
- 1 tsp kosher salt.
- Black pepper.
- 3/4 cup T1 lemon-tahini base.
- 3 tbsp H1 chopped herb mix.
- 1/2 cup CV1 quick pickles.
- Sumac or chili flake.
- Bread/toast if available. The master dough will be ready Wednesday.

Session order:

1. Start master dough.
2. Wash/dry herbs and make H1, H2, H3.
3. Make CV1 quick pickles, CV2 snack sticks, and CV3 salted salad if serving tonight.
4. Make T1 lemon-tahini base.
5. Make strawberry chia jars.
6. Roast dinner.

Dinner method:

1. Heat oven to 450 F.
2. Toss cabbage with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Roast cabbage 20 minutes.
4. Add asparagus tossed with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt.
5. Roast 8 to 12 minutes more, until asparagus is just tender and cabbage is charred at edges.
6. Plate with lemon-tahini, herbs, pickles, and sumac/chili.

Save:

- 1 cabbage/asparagus lunch box.
- Remaining tahini base.
- Pickles.
- Raw snack sticks.
- Chia jars.
- Dough in fridge.

## Wednesday: Asparagus, Pea, Herb, And Feta Frittata

Dinner output:

- Dinner for 2.
- 1 lunch portion.
- Full chickpea batch cooked and split.
- Dough divided into week portions.

Ingredients:

- 8 eggs.
- 1/4 cup milk or yogurt.
- 1/2 bunch asparagus, sliced 1 inch thick.
- 1 cup peas or snap peas, chopped if large.
- 3 scallions, sliced.
- 1/4 cup H1 chopped herb mix.
- 3 to 4 oz feta.
- 1 tbsp olive oil.
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
- Black pepper.
- CV3 salted cucumber/radish salad or CV1 pickles.

Session order:

1. Start Instant Pot chickpeas.
2. Divide master dough into balls.
3. Make frittata.
4. Split chickpeas once cool enough.

Frittata method:

1. Heat oven to 400 F.
2. Whisk eggs, milk/yogurt, salt, pepper, and half the herbs.
3. In an oven-safe skillet, saute scallions and asparagus in olive oil for 3 minutes.
4. Add peas and cook 1 minute.
5. Pour in eggs.
6. Scatter feta over top.
7. Cook on stove 2 minutes until edges set.
8. Transfer to oven 8 to 12 minutes, until just set.
9. Finish with remaining herbs.

Chickpea handling:

1. Cook 1 lb dry chickpeas in Instant Pot.
2. Drain, reserving aquafaba.
3. Split into CP1, CP2, CP3, CP4, and AQ1.
4. Refrigerate uncovered until steam stops, then cover.

Save:

- 1 to 2 frittata squares for Thursday lunch/snack box.
- CP1 hummus chickpeas.
- CP2 crispy chickpeas, drying on towel if roasting Friday.
- CP3 whole chickpeas.
- Aquafaba.
- Dough balls.

## Friday: Miso-Tahini Asparagus And Pea Noodles

Dinner output:

- Dinner for 2.
- 1 cold noodle lunch portion.
- Crispy chickpeas or pita chips.
- Saturday Ooni dough checked.

Ingredients:

- 10 to 12 oz noodles, pasta, or gnocchi.
- 1 bunch asparagus, sliced.
- 1 cup peas or snap peas.
- 2 scallions, sliced.
- 2 tbsp H1 chopped herb mix.
- Sesame seeds, optional.

Miso-tahini sauce:

- 1/2 cup T1 lemon-tahini base.
- 2 tbsp white or yellow miso.
- 1 tbsp soy sauce.
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice.
- 1 tsp maple syrup or honey.
- 1 tsp sesame oil.
- Chili crisp or chili oil to taste.
- 1/4 to 1/2 cup hot noodle water.

Session order:

1. Pull any dough that needs checking; make sure Saturday pizza balls are covered and healthy.
2. Start boiling water.
3. Roast crispy chickpeas or pita chips if using oven.
4. Cook noodles/pasta/gnocchi.
5. Blanch asparagus and peas in the same water during the last 1 to 2 minutes.
6. Toss with sauce.

Method:

1. Whisk sauce ingredients except noodle water.
2. Cook noodles until just tender.
3. Add asparagus and peas near the end.
4. Reserve noodle water, then drain.
5. Toss hot noodles/veg with sauce, loosening with noodle water.
6. Finish with herbs, scallions, sesame, and chili crisp.

Save:

- 1 cold noodle lunch box.
- Crispy chickpeas.
- Remaining hummus/herb yogurt.
- Saturday Ooni dough balls.

## What The Automated Planner Should Learn

- Herbs are not one ingredient. They have storage-state edges: washed whole leaves, chopped mix, stems, and sauce.
- Dough is a carrier with a fermentation window, not a single recipe.
- Crunchy vegetables have quality clocks. Pickled radish/cucumber can last; salted cucumber salad cannot.
- Chickpeas should split into hummus, crispy, whole, aquafaba, and buffer states.
- Ooni firing should always produce dinner plus at least one future state.
- The weekly plan should specify quantities and containers, not just recipe names.
